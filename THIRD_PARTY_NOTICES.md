# Third-party UI and brand assets

The components in `src/components/ui` are adapted from [shadcn/ui](https://github.com/shadcn-ui/ui), under the MIT license. Navigation indicators were changed to text controls for FlowTrack.

The subscription SVGs in `public/logos` are copied from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org), distributed under CC0. Brand names and logos belong to their respective owners. These assets identify subscriptions entered by the user; FlowTrack is not affiliated with these services.


PDF statement reading uses Mozilla PDF.js 5.6.205, Apache-2.0, vendored in public/vendor/pdfjs. Source: https://github.com/mozilla/pdf.js. The license and original copyright notices are retained. PDF.js loads only when a PDF is uploaded; all processing happens on the device.
